Images in these formats, in pages and on their own:

 MacPaint
 PICT, version 1 and 2 (version 2: its images only)
 XBM, X10 and X11
 BMP: 1, 4, 8, or 24 bits, RLE4 and RLE8
 Netpbm: PBM, PGM, and PPM
 PNG: every kind, with transparency and interlacing

PNG: Transparency is not blended. CRC checks optional, see settings.

Each format can be turned off from the Settings page, under Formats.
